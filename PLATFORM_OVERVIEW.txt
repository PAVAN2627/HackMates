WHAT IS HACKMATES?
──────────────────
HackMates is a web platform built for students and developers in India who
participate in hackathons. It helps them discover hackathons, build teams,
communicate with teammates, and track their reputation — all in one place.

Live at: https://hackmates-mu.vercel.app
Built by: PAVAN MALI (NoobcodersIND)


WHO IS IT FOR?
──────────────
HackMates is built for:

  • College students who want to participate in hackathons but don't have a
    team yet.

  • Developers, designers, and creators who are looking for people with
    complementary skills to build something together.

  • Hackathon organizers who want to post their event and attract participants.

  • Anyone who has been burned by unreliable teammates and wants a way to
    find people they can actually trust.

In short: if you've ever thought "I want to join a hackathon but I don't know
anyone to team up with" — HackMates is for you.


THE PROBLEM
───────────
Hackathons are one of the best ways to learn, build, and get noticed. But
there's a frustrating gap that almost every participant faces:

  1. FINDING TEAMMATES IS HARD
     Most people find teammates through WhatsApp groups, college friends, or
     random Discord servers. There's no structured way to search for someone
     with the exact skills you need — like "I need a React developer who is
     available on weekends and wants to build something in AI."

  2. YOU DON'T KNOW WHO TO TRUST
     You invite someone to your team, they say yes, and then they ghost you
     two days before the submission. This is extremely common. There's no
     accountability system — anyone can join and disappear.

  3. HACKATHON DISCOVERY IS SCATTERED
     Hackathons are announced across Instagram, LinkedIn, college notice
     boards, and random websites. There's no single place to find them all,
     filter by mode (online/offline), or see which ones are still open.

  4. TEAM COMMUNICATION IS MESSY
     Teams end up using a mix of WhatsApp, Discord, Google Docs, and email.
     There's no dedicated space for hackathon-specific team chat, announcements
     from organizers, or project coordination.

  5. NO WAY TO REPORT BAD ACTORS
     If someone scams you, harasses you, or fakes their profile, there's
     nothing you can do. No reporting system, no moderation, no consequences.


HOW HACKMATES SOLVES IT
────────────────────────

  PROBLEM 1 → Smart Profile Search
  Users build detailed profiles with skills, experience level, work style,
  availability, and location. You can search and filter by any of these.
  The platform also shows a "Synergy Score" — a compatibility percentage
  calculated from your work goals, schedule, and commitment level — so you
  can instantly see who you'd work well with.

  PROBLEM 2 → Reliability Badge System
  Every user has a trust score (0–100) and a badge: Newbie, Reliable,
  Finisher, or Legend. Badges are earned by completing hackathons and getting
  rated by teammates. Before you invite someone, you can see their track
  record. This creates real accountability.

  PROBLEM 3 → Hackathon Discovery
  Any user can post a hackathon. All hackathons are listed in one place with
  filters for mode (online/offline/hybrid), required skills, location, and
  status (open/closed). No more hunting across five different platforms.

  PROBLEM 4 → Built-in Communication
  HackMates has direct messaging (1-on-1), team chat (for your specific team),
  and hackathon-wide group chat. Organizers can post announcements that go to
  all participants via the platform and email. Everything is in one place.

  PROBLEM 5 → Report & Moderation System
  Users can report others for scam, harassment, fake profiles, ghosting, or
  inappropriate behavior. Reports include a description and up to 5 screenshot
  proofs. An admin team reviews every report and can block users, removing
  all their content from the platform.


KEY FEATURES AT A GLANCE
─────────────────────────

  • Hackathon discovery and posting (any user can post)
  • Team creation and member invitations with email notifications
  • Live user search by name, skill, or email
  • Synergy Score — compatibility matching based on work style
  • Reliability badges — trust system built on real participation history
  • Direct messages, team chat, and hackathon group chat
  • Organizer announcements with email alerts to all members
  • Off-platform teams — for hackathons not listed on HackMates
  • Report User — 3-step reporting with image proof uploads
  • Admin dashboard — analytics, user management, report moderation
  • AI Assistant — Gemini-powered chatbot for hackathon guidance
  • GitHub profile verification badge
  • Dark/light theme, fully mobile responsive


WHY IT'S WORTH USING
─────────────────────

Most hackathon participants waste hours finding teammates, lose trust in
people who ghost them, and miss events because they didn't hear about them
in time. HackMates removes all of that friction.

  • You find the right people faster — not just "someone available" but
    someone whose skills, schedule, and goals actually match yours.

  • You build with people you can trust — the reliability system means
    consistent performers are visible and rewarded.

  • You stay organized — one platform for discovery, communication,
    announcements, and team management.

  • You're protected — if someone behaves badly, there's a real reporting
    and moderation system with consequences.

  • You grow your reputation — every hackathon you complete improves your
    badge and trust score, which makes future team invitations easier.

For Indian college students especially, where hackathon culture is huge but
infrastructure for team formation is almost nonexistent, HackMates fills a
real and practical gap.


THE BIGGER PICTURE
──────────────────
India has one of the largest and most active hackathon communities in the
world. Thousands of events happen every year — from college-level to national
competitions like Smart India Hackathon. But the ecosystem around team
formation, trust, and communication is still primitive.

HackMates is an attempt to build that infrastructure — to make it as easy to
find a hackathon teammate as it is to find a ride on Ola, or a freelancer on
Fiverr. The goal is not just to help individuals win hackathons, but to raise
the overall quality of collaboration in India's tech community.
