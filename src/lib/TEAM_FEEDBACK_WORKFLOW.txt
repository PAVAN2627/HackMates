TEAM FEEDBACK WORKFLOW - How It Works
======================================

PROBLEM SOLVED:
- In large hackathons (100-120+ participants), having everyone rate everyone is impractical
- Solution: Team-based feedback system with 3-stage lifecycle

HACKATHON LIFECYCLE (3 STAGES):
--------------------------------

1. **OPEN** - Registration Phase
   - Students can join the hackathon
   - Creator forms teams and adds members
   - General chat is active
   - Join/Leave buttons work

2. **IN PROGRESS** - Working Phase
   - No new joins allowed (button disabled)
   - Teams work on their projects
   - General chat is still active
   - Creator can still manage teams

3. **COMPLETED** - Feedback Phase
   - Chat is closed (read-only)
   - No team changes allowed
   - "Rate Teammates" button appears
   - Team members can rate each other

WORKFLOW:
---------

STAGE 1: OPEN (Registration)
-----------------------------
1. Creator creates hackathon (status: "open")
2. Students join the hackathon (100-120+ people)
3. All joined members appear in "Members" tab
4. Creator goes to "Teams" tab and creates teams
5. Creator adds members to teams from "Members" tab:
   - Click "View Profile" to see full details
   - Click "Add to Team" to assign to a team
6. Members in teams show green "In Team" badge
7. Creator clicks "Remove Non-Team Members" to clean up
8. Only selected team members remain

STAGE 2: IN PROGRESS (Working)
-------------------------------
1. Creator clicks "Start" button (status changes to "in-progress")
2. Join/Leave buttons become disabled
3. Teams work on their projects
4. General chat remains active for collaboration
5. Creator can still manage teams if needed

STAGE 3: COMPLETED (Feedback)
------------------------------
1. Creator clicks "Complete" button (status changes to "completed")
2. General chat becomes read-only with message:
   "This hackathon has been completed. Chat is now closed."
3. "Rate Teammates" button appears for all team members
4. Each team member rates only their teammates (3-4 people)
5. Feedback updates reliability badges automatically

STATUS BUTTON CHANGES:
----------------------
- When "Open": Shows "Start" button (blue)
- When "In Progress": Shows "Complete" button (blue)
- When "Completed": Shows "Reopen" button (gray) - to restart if needed

BENEFITS:
---------
✓ Scalable: Works for hackathons of any size
✓ Manageable: Only rate 3-4 teammates, not 100+ people
✓ Fair: Only committed team members participate
✓ Clean: Non-selected members are removed automatically
✓ Organized: Clear team structure for collaboration
✓ Flexible: Creator can add/remove members as needed
✓ Controlled: Three-stage lifecycle prevents chaos
✓ Professional: Proper workflow from start to finish
