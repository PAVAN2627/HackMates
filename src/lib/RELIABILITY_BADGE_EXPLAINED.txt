RELIABILITY BADGE SYSTEM - How It Works
========================================

IMPORTANT: Your badge is based on feedback YOU RECEIVE, not feedback you give!

HOW IT WORKS:
-------------

1. YOU COMPLETE A HACKATHON
   - You join a hackathon
   - Creator adds you to a team
   - Hackathon is marked as "Completed"

2. TEAMMATES RATE YOU
   - Your teammates click "Rate Teammates"
   - They rate YOUR contribution (1-5 stars)
   - They mark if you contributed or ghosted
   - This feedback goes to YOUR profile

3. YOUR BADGE UPDATES
   - System counts unique hackathons where you received feedback
   - Calculates your completion rate and average rating
   - Updates your badge level

BADGE LEVELS:
-------------
🌱 Newbie (0 hackathons completed)
   - No feedback received yet
   - Default for new users

✓ Reliable (1+ hackathons, 60+ score)
   - Completed at least 1 hackathon
   - Teammates confirmed you contributed
   - Good ratings from team

⭐ Finisher (3+ hackathons, 80+ score)
   - Completed 3+ hackathons
   - High contribution rate
   - Excellent ratings

👑 Legend (10+ hackathons, 95+ score)
   - Completed 10+ hackathons
   - Nearly perfect record
   - Outstanding ratings

EXAMPLE SCENARIO:
-----------------

Hackathon: "AI Challenge 2024"
Team: You, Alice, Bob, Charlie

After hackathon completes:
- Alice rates YOU → Feedback goes to YOUR profile
- Bob rates YOU → Feedback goes to YOUR profile  
- Charlie rates YOU → Feedback goes to YOUR profile
- YOU rate Alice → Feedback goes to ALICE's profile
- YOU rate Bob → Feedback goes to BOB's profile
- YOU rate Charlie → Feedback goes to CHARLIE's profile

Result:
- YOUR profile shows 3 feedbacks received (from Alice, Bob, Charlie)
- YOUR badge updates based on those 3 feedbacks
- The feedback YOU gave appears on THEIR profiles, not yours

WHY THIS DESIGN?
----------------
✓ Your badge reflects what OTHERS think of you
✓ Prevents self-rating manipulation
✓ Shows your reputation to potential teammates
✓ Builds trust in the community

CURRENT STATUS:
---------------
If you see "🌱 Newbie - 0 projects completed":
→ You haven't received any feedback yet
→ Ask your teammates to rate you!
→ Once they do, your badge will update automatically

If you gave feedback to someone:
→ That feedback is on THEIR profile
→ It doesn't affect YOUR badge
→ You need to RECEIVE feedback to upgrade your badge
